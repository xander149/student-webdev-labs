Chrome Dino Game

The Chrome Dino Game was released in 2014 by Google. It appears when users lose internet connection in the Chrome browser. Players control a small dinosaur that runs endlessly, jumping over obstacles like cacti and dodging flying enemies. It's simple, fun, and addictive.
